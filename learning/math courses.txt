Some math textbooks I read at Princeton as a math major:

1. Gunning, An Introduction to Analysis
2. Axler, Linear algebra done right
3. Armstrong, Groups and Symmetry
4. Hatcher, Algebraic topology (I haven’t seen a better and more interesting textbook in my life.)
5. Koralov, Sinai, Theory of Probability and Random Processes
6. Stein, Complex analysis
7. Munkres, Topology (my fav!)
8. Stefan H. M. van Zwam, Combinatorial mathematics: http://www.matroidunion.org/stefan/pdf/notes.pdf
9. Shafarevich, Basic algebraic geometry I

In my first semester as a graduate student at Harvard, I read “Differential Geometry: Bundles, Connections, Metrics and Curvature” by Taubes and a probability textbook by a Harvard professor that hasn’t been published yet (but the class lecture notes are available online: https://www.ekzhang.com/assets/pdf/Stat_210_Notes.pdf)
