Academic Journey

At 17, Katya moved to the US alone to study Mathematics at Princeton University. This experience shaped her perspective on education and inspired her to help others access education.

Content Creator

As a first-generation immigrant in the US and a woman in STEM, Katya shares her journey on social media with genuine candor, focusing on academic life, productivity, and personal growth.

Speaker

Katya is passionate about increasing access to education and empowering underrepresented students in STEM. Katya’s speaking and outreach work includes keynote addresses and talks at various STEM events such as the Northeastern Graduate Women in Science and Engineering workshop, the Girls in Engineering and Robotics Symposium, HacKnight at BB&N High School, ENYGMMa at CUNY.

Mentor

Katya coaches students 1-1 to get into their dream undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs.

Collaborations

Katya works with leading AI and education-focused such as Jenni AI, Unriddle, and acely.ai to create educational content and improve access to learning resources.

Beyond Academics

Outside of work, Katya enjoys dancing, travelling, unicycling, listening to music, and spending time with my dog
