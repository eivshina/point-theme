Publications/preprints

TESS transit timing of hundreds of hot Jupiters

Ivshina E., Winn J.

The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series, 2022

I searched for evidence of orbital period changes in 382 planets. My contribution was developing a parallelized code to fit transit models to TESS light curves and scrape timing data from ArXiv, which helped us discover transit timing variations and detect a new planet, NGTS-11c

Doughnut or Mickey Mouse? Detecting Toroidal Structure in Data through Persistent Cup-Length

Ivshina E., Anikeeva G., Zhou L.

arXiv, 2025

This paper introduces the first practical implementation of persistent cup-length and applies it to detect toroidal structure in grid cell populations.
