Katya Ivshina is a mathematician, machine learning researcher, and creative based in London.
Educated at Princeton (B.A. Mathematics, with honors) and Harvard (M.S. Applied Mathematics), Katya has spent the last seven years at the frontier of research — publishing across machine learning, topology, neuroscience, and astrophysics, where she helped discover a new exoplanet. She has researched at Harvard, MIT, and Apple, presented at international forums from Stockholm to Tokyo, and holds an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.
Beyond the lab, Katya has built a platform of over 100,000 followers and more than 30 million views, becoming a trusted voice for students, immigrants, and women navigating STEM. As a first-generation immigrant who arrived in the US at seventeen, she speaks candidly about ambition, access, and the courage to build a life on one's own terms — and has carried that message to audiences at Imperial College London, CUNY, and beyond.
This summer marks a new chapter. After a decade defined almost entirely by science, Katya is finally giving space to the creative disciplines she has long pursued alongside her research — dance, photography, filmmaking, and modeling — and to the work of building something of her own.
